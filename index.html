About This System

The ULSU × MyExperience Leadership Reporting System is the formal channel through which ULSU-registered club members document their leadership contributions each month. Reports submitted here are reviewed by the VP of Student Life and used to validate MyExperience credit for the students involved.

MyExperience is the University of Lethbridge's co-curricular recognition program. It acknowledges the time, skill, and effort students invest in campus life beyond the classroom — including club leadership/volunteerism. To receive recognition, your club's activity must be reported accurately and on time each month.

This form takes roughly 5–10 minutes to complete. Once submitted, your email client will open with a pre-filled receipt addressed to the VP of Student Life. Simply click Send to finalize your submission.

Before You Begin
✓

Data Accuracy

Ensure the following are accurate before submitting. Errors may delay your recognition or require resubmission.

  • Student IDs match university records exactly
  • Hours committed may be rounded up to the nearest whole hour
  • Position titles are consistent with your club's constitution
  • Reporting period dates cover the correct calendar month
  • Contact email is monitored and belongs to the reporter
⏱

Submission Deadline

Monthly — Last Day of Each Month

Reports must be submitted before the last day of the reporting month to be counted toward that period's MyExperience recognition.

Late submissions may be accepted at the discretion of the VP of Student Life but are not guaranteed recognition for the missed period. If in doubt, submit early.
